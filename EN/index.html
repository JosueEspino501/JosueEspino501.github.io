About me
A career change with purpose

My career started in architecture, designing plans and supervising construction sites where every decision had to be precise and backed by real field information.

Over time I realized that what I enjoyed most was not the building itself, but the analysis behind it: interpreting data, detecting deviations and making decisions based on evidence.

That realization led me to data science. I completed the TripleTen bootcamp, where I worked with Python, machine learning models (LightGBM, CatBoost, XGBoost) and time series on projects with complex datasets.

Today my goal is to contribute to teams where data is at the center of decision-making.
